How to play

  1. Move with the Arrow keys or W A S D. Press Space to dash. On a phone, use the buttons.
  2. You can't go outside without boots! Find your space boots, then run to the airlock and step onto the alien grass.
  3. Your Sunlight drains indoors. Stand in a sunbeam by a window to refill it. If it reaches 0, you die.
  4. TVs pull you in when they're on. Walk past them while the screen is off. Couches shake, then charge in a straight line, so step out of the way.
  5. Dust bunnies stick to you and slow you down. Dash to shake them off, but 4 of them will bury you. Robot vacuums can't be stopped, and toy bricks on the floor hurt to step on.
  6. Points: +20 for each new room, +100 for the boots, +5 for each shaken-off bunny, a big bonus for escaping, and extra for escaping fast. If you die, press Play Again.
